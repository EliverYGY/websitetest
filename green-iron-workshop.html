About the workshop

A shared response to the global shift toward low-carbon steel

China and Australia hold strong complementary strengths in ironmaking and steelmaking, iron ore resources, renewable energy and research capability. Following a start high, stay focused approach, this first Workshop and the associated Green Iron Young Scientists Mentorship Program bring together academicians, industry leaders and emerging researchers to focus on green ironmaking, low-carbon metallurgy, resource efficiency and industry transformation.

Co-convened by Academician Xinping Mao and Professor Jianliang Zhang (China) and Professor Yansong Shen, FTSE (Australia), the Workshop is intended to alternate between Australia and China in future years.

Program highlights

Three core components

01

Keynotes & discussion

High-level presentations from distinguished Academy Fellows and experts in green iron and low-carbon metallurgy.

02

Young Scientists Mentorship

An open call and selection process pairing early- and mid-career researchers with senior mentors from both countries.

03

Partnership announcements

Signing of the Australia–China Green Iron Research Platform MoU, launch of the Iron & Steel Green and Low-Carbon Special Issue, and academic honours.
